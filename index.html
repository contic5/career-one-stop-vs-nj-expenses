Career One Stop vs NJ Expenses

About

This project graphs CareerOneStop job salary data against New Jersey 1 bedroom apartment expenses from Apartments.com.

There are five main charts:

  1. Apartment Rent and Recommended Monthly Income: Bar plot that displays how much an apartment type costs per month and the recommended monthly income for that apartment.
  2. Apartment Rent and Recommended Yearly Income: Bar plot that displays how much an apartment type costs per year and the recommended yearly income for that apartment.
  3. 2025 Median Monthly Incomes: Bar plot with job median monthly incomes and horizontal lines displaying the recommended monthly income for apartment types.
  4. 2025 Median Yearly Incomes: Bar plot with job median yearly incomes and horizontal lines displaying the recommended yearly income for apartment types.
  5. Apartment Payment (Target Percent): Bar plot displaying the recommended apartment payment based on median job wage and taget percent. Horizontal lines also display the apartment payment for apartment types.
